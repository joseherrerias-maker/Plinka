# Pluki Build 9 — Share a song as a video

## Included
- Song menu → Export video → Create video → Preview → Save to Photos. Vertical 720×1280 or landscape 1280×720, 30 fps MP4 with H.264 video and audio. Uses the approved stickers, scrolling staff, song title and Pluki branding; editor controls are omitted.
- One complete play-through, regardless of Loop. Audio is generated by the same engine and gain as playback; sound tails are retained. Frame positions derive from song time to avoid timer drift.
- Photos add-only permission is requested only when saving. Denial preserves the preview for retry. Save success is shown only after Photos completes. Export supports cancellation, cleans temporary files, and cancels when backgrounded; preview pauses when inactive. Keep Pluki open during creation.
- Extend sheet adds four measures at a time, up to 32 measures. Saved songs and drafts preserve length, including late notes. Existing saves remain compatible.
- Six longer editable samples (12–24 measures), including original songs and traditional/classical arrangements. Each plays once by default.
- Home New song starts immediately. In-song New song and opening saved songs retain their unsaved-change checks. Samples open immediately.
- Settings displays Build 9. Marketing version 0.7.0; Codemagic continues managing the upload build number separately.

## Build
Use main and the single **Pluki Build 9 - TestFlight and iPad ZIP** workflow (`pluki-ios-dual-export`). Existing signing identities, TestFlight publishing and iPad ZIP export are unchanged. No paid build is triggered automatically.

## Verification
Run saved-song checks, unsigned iOS compile, orientation/release-label/Photos permission and sound-resource checks. A small opt-in Debug simulator smoke check renders vertical and landscape movies with recorded animal audio and a note beyond the original sheet length, checks media tracks/duration/dimensions and cancellation, and captures preview frames. It does not access Photos or run UI automation.

Device acceptance still required: export a short song and a long sample in both formats; preview and save to Photos; listen for synchronization and final sound tails; check portrait/landscape appearance, cancellation/retry, permission denial/retry, storage failures and backgrounding. Verify saved songs, longer sheets, home/menu and smooth playback on iPhone and iPad. Photos authorization/import must be tested on a real device.

Build 8 remains the user-approved device baseline. Archive Build 9 only after the user confirms successful signed compilation; retain the latest three compiled archives.
