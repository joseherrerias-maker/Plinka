Pluki privacy policy
Updated October 26, 2026

Music stays on your device
Pluki creates and plays music on your iPhone or iPad. Pluki does not send your songs, song names, or usage information to the developer. It does not have accounts, advertisements, tracking, third-party analytics, or an online song-sharing service.

Saved songs and drafts
Song names, notes, tempo, loop settings, saved projects and the current draft are stored locally. Open song lets you delete saved projects. Starting a new song replaces the working draft. Deleting Pluki can remove its local data. Your device's backup settings may include app data; those backups are managed by Apple, not by Pluki.

Video and Photos
Video creation happens on your device using the included sounds and artwork. Pluki does not record your microphone, camera or screen. The song title is included in the exported video, so avoid personal information in titles you plan to share.

A parent check is required before first requesting Photos permission. Once add-only access is granted, videos save directly without another parent check. Pluki requests add-only Photos permission when you choose to save; it does not read your existing photos or videos. You can decline permission and continue composing. You can change permission in device Settings. Videos already saved to Photos are managed and deleted in Photos, and may sync through iCloud Photos according to your Apple settings.

Temporary files
Pluki creates temporary audio and video files while exporting. It removes them when an export is cancelled, fails, or its preview is closed during normal operation. Files left by an unexpected app termination may remain in the app's temporary storage until the system removes them or the app is deleted.

Children and families
Pluki does not ask for a child's name, age, location, email address or other profile information. Parent-check answers are checked on the device and are not stored or sent anywhere. The parent check is an interaction safeguard, not identity verification or consent to collect personal data.

Apple services
Apple handles app distribution, purchases, optional device backups and Photos services under its own terms and privacy practices. Pluki does not receive your payment details. Feedback you voluntarily send through TestFlight is handled by Apple and may be provided to the developer.

Contact
A parent or guardian can contact support@pebblesoftapps.com for help or privacy questions. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you include so we can respond. Please do not send children's personal information. Support email is handled through Microsoft 365 from GoDaddy; it is separate from the offline app.
