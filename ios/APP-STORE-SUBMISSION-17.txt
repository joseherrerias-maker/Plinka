DESCRIPTION
Make a little music with pluki!

Turn playful sounds into your own songs. Pick an instrument, animal or fun sound, then tap the music sheet to place a colorful sticker. Move it up or down to change pitch, or sideways to change when it plays.

A whole world of sounds
Explore 19 sounds, from piano and guitar to animal calls and playful effects. Mix them together and hear what happens.

Start with a song
Try six sample tunes, including familiar melodies and original pluki arrangements. Play them, change the sounds, and make your own version.

Keep creating
The sheet grows as you add notes, up to 500 numbered beats. Adjust the tempo, loop your tune, and use undo and redo while you experiment.

Save your favorites
Name and save songs on your device so you can open them and keep composing later.

Your song, now a video
Create an animated video of your music sheet in portrait or landscape, then save it to Photos to share with family and friends.

Simple from the start
No account, ads, in-app purchases or subscription. Music creation works offline. Saved projects stay on the device; cloud project sync is not included.

APP REVIEW NOTES
No account or demo credentials are required. Choose New song or Samples. Select a sound, then tap the music sheet to add notes. Press Play to hear the song.

The Song menu contains the song title/pencil for Rename, Save song, Open song, Samples, Export video, and Help & About. Songs are saved locally. Rename updates the active title; Save stores the project. Clear notes asks for confirmation, and Undo can restore the cleared notes.

To export, use a song with notes, select Export video, choose a format and Create video. Save to Photos presents a parent check before the first add-only Photos permission request. Tap the four number words as digits in the displayed order using the built-in keypad, then Continue. The challenge is generated each time; there is no fixed code. Once Photos access is granted, later saves proceed directly. Declining access leaves composition available.

Help & About provides offline help/privacy, sound credits and Contact & website. Every outgoing contact/website action requires a fresh parent check. The app does not require a microphone, camera or reading existing photos.
