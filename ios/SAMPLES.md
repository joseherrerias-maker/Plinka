# Pluki release sample arrangements

These built-in projects are editable arrangements using Pluki's own playback engine and existing credited sound assets. They do not embed commercial song recordings. Existing saved copies remain unchanged; stable sample IDs and titles are retained.

| Sample | Sheet | Arrangement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star | 12 measures | Complete A–B–A melody, piano and bells, sparse guitar accents |
| Frère Jacques | 16 measures | Two complete passes, flute then xylophone; the final phrase's low dominant is raised an octave to fit the staff |
| Ode to Joy | 16 measures | Opening theme and answer repeated with an instrument variation; not the complete symphonic work |
| Old MacDonald | 24 measures | Two refrain/break/refrain verses, with cow then chicken call-and-response breaks |
| Pluki Pop | 16 measures | Original A–B–A–A arrangement with a xylophone section and light drums |
| Animal Parade | 18 measures | Original alternating phrases, six animal sounds, and a four-note bell ending |

One measure is eight half-beat steps. Samples play once by default; listeners may enable Loop. Sheet length is editing space; audio ends naturally after the final note's sound decays. Existing sounds do not implement arbitrary sustained-note lengths: timing here describes note onsets and spacing.

Checks cover pitch/time bounds, distinct note IDs, no overlapping editable cells, JSON round trips including length, and saved-copy independence. Listening on a physical device is still required before release. Baby Shark and modern pop-song compositions are not included.
