# Pluki Build 5

Prepared from compiled Build 4 plus its simple build-label correction.

- New simple, friendly transparent stickers follow the supplied puppy/drum
  references: clean rounded shapes, tiny faces, and minimal shading. Detailed
  glossy renders were rejected and are not part of the build.
- One continuous tray contains 8 instruments, 6 animals, and 5 fun sounds.
  Instruments / Animals / Fun remain visible as scrolling shortcuts. The
  active category follows the leading visible sticker while swiping.
- Puppy uses a recorded bark. Chicken, Pig, Cow, and Frog use short recorded
  animal calls. Recordings are bundled offline and change pitch with the staff.
- Bubbles, slide Whistle, and cartoon Laser join Sparkle and Boing.
- Sample playback includes full note tails, edge fades, and cached audio.
- Settings shows only Build 5; Apple metadata is 0.4.0 with Codemagic's counter.
- Sound credits and recording licenses are available in Settings and Sounds.
