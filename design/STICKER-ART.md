# Pluki Build 5 sticker direction

The user's SoundSprout reference is authoritative: simple friendly cartoon
symbols, tiny oval eyes and curved smiles, broad clean colors, minimal shading.
The supplied Puppy and Drums crops were used directly as image references.
Detailed glossy toy renders were rejected and are not production assets.

Production assets: `ios/Plinka/Assets.xcassets/<sound>.imageset/<sound>.png`.
All 19 stickers use transparent PNGs, sized to 384 pixels for the app.
Artwork was generated using the built-in image generation tool, then resized
for delivery without additional stylization. Per-sound briefs are recorded below.
The existing SVGs are historical V2 artwork;
the legacy generator now only updates the background to protect new stickers.

cream face, caramel floppy ears and side patches, small upright oval eyes,
small dark nose, and a simple curved smile. No fur, tongue, realistic texture,
extra tufts, glossy reflections, or card/label. Minimal soft shading.

Drums prompt: reproduce only the small smiling drum from the supplied crop.
Preserve thick red rims, cream drumhead and face panel, tiny oval eyes, curved
smile, orange cheeks, simple blue side accents, and two yellow sticks. No
realistic hardware, elaborate cords, glossy reflections, or card/label.
